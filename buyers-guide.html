Market Overview

The Market Has Fundamentally Changed

For many years, enterprise IT procurement operated in a buyer-friendly environment. Hardware was widely available, competition drove prices downward, and teams had flexibility to compare options.

Today that landscape has changed dramatically. Demand for compute, storage, and memory has surged driven by AI expansion, while supply chains have struggled to keep pace.

The result is a fundamental shift to a sellers market. Pricing is less predictable, lead times are longer, and immediate availability no longer applies to many components.

Enterprise buyers increasingly encounter allocation programs, constrained supply pipelines, and rapidly fluctuating component costs.

Key shift: Today the most pressing question is no longer about price — it is Can we source hardware within our required timeframe?

Strategies for a Constrained Market

1

Lifecycle Extension via TPM

TPM programs let organizations continue operating critical infrastructure while delaying large capex saving 30-85% vs OEM rates.

2

Hybrid Infrastructure Models

Combining on-premise with cloud resources allows distributing workloads and maintaining flexibility without relying on immediate hardware procurement.

3

Hardware Rental Programs

Short-term rentals offer practical solutions for compute-intensive projects without permanent purchases during volatile pricing periods.

4

Certified Refurbished Hardware

Refurbished enterprise hardware offers a cost-effective alternative with tested components backed by warranty and replacement assurances.

5

Cloud Migration

Cloud platforms can provide immediate compute resources that may be difficult to obtain on-premise during supply shortages.

6

Hardware Redeployment

Redeploy underutilized hardware elsewhere to maximize value of existing investments and extend effective service life.

Combine lifecycle extension, hybrid deployments, flexible sourcing, and strategic partnerships for the most resilient procurement strategy.

Understanding Gray Market Risk

Gray Market Trade-Offs

Gray market products may lack OEM serial numbers restricting access to support, firmware updates, and licensing. For core infrastructure like GPUs and storage, hidden risks far outweigh upfront savings. Always verify serial numbers and confirm support eligibility.
